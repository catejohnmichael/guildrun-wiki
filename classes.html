Classes, Guilds, Rank-ups

Classes

Warrior HeroClass_1

Warriors use Attack to deal damage with auto attacks and active abilities.

Warrior Mechanics: Crit, Rush, Shields.

Primary stat: Attack · Secondary: Crit, MaxHealth, AttackSpeed

Heroes: Reyna, Logan, Tilly, Kai

Tank HeroClass_2

Tanks use Defense to protect their allies and disrupt enemies.

Tank Mechanics: Stall, Poison, Frost.

Primary stat: Defense · Secondary: MaxHealth, Magic, ManaRegen

Heroes: Zuri, Rowan, Skorn, Pimenta

Vanguard HeroClass_3

Vanguards use Max HP to absorb and dish out damage.

Vanguard Mechanics: Rush, Shields, Burn.

Primary stat: MaxHealth · Secondary: Defense, AttackSpeed, Attack

Heroes: Niklas, Rowan, Rip, Ming

Assassin HeroClass_4

Assassins use Crit to deal bursts of damage with their auto attacks and active abilities.

Assassin Mechanics: Crit, Shards, Poison, Rush, Stealth.

Primary stat: Crit · Secondary: Attack, Magic, Defense

Heroes: Hoyoung, Yuuna, Zuri, Dragomir

Duelist HeroClass_5

Duelists use Attack Speed to rapidly auto attack.

Duelist Mechanics: Stall, Rush, Debuffs, Crit, Shards.

Primary stat: AttackSpeed · Secondary: Crit, Attack, MaxHealth

Heroes: Irini, Nyx, Tilly, Karsu

Mystic HeroClass_6

Mystics use Mana Regen to repeatedly cast their active ability, aiding allies and disrupting enemies.

Mystic Mechanics: Shields, Shards, Debuffs, Stall.

Primary stat: ManaRegen · Secondary: Magic, AttackSpeed, Defense

Heroes: Pollen, Fiona, Gustav, Grace

Mage HeroClass_7

Mages use Magic to deal damage and disrupt enemies with their active abilities.

Mage Mechanics: Stall, Burn, Frost.

Primary stat: Magic · Secondary: ManaRegen, Crit, MaxHealth

Heroes: Aria, Funke, Ratna, Sal

Guilds

Frontline Guild_1 (Sporty)

Stronger every break.

They believe the only way to close the rifts is to overpower them, and they've turned that conviction into a performance brand. Teams train and compete like athletes, chasing a personal best with every break.

Heroes: Irini, Reyna, Logan, Grace

The Hunt Guild_2 (Street)

When the call comes, we hunt.

A guild of Awakened professionals built for the chase, first through the door and closing in before a rift can breach do real damage. They hunt to win: the monster, the trophy, and the reputation that follows.

Heroes: Hoyoung, Yuuna, Nyx, Ming, Kai

L'Héritage Guild_3 (Luxury)

Excellence is a legacy.

A prestige guild that recruits two kinds of breakers: heirs to old money, and rising stars famous enough to be worth the investment. It trains elite, media-ready breakers and moves easily through the circles where wealth, fame, and power overlap.

Heroes: Zuri, Aria, Tilly, Ratna

A Temple Guild_4 (Punk)

No gods. No masters.

A guild that believes the Shards & relics locked inside the rifts belongs to everyone, not to whoever can buy the rights to it. They fight to put those treasures in the hands of ordinary people.

Heroes: Skorn, Funke, Rip, Karsu

Gaia Project Guild_5 (Bohemian)

Fight for tomorrow, not just today.

A community-driven guild that treats the rifts as living phenomena to be understood. Its breakers study each one closely and work to restore the natural balance around it, often staying long after the fighting is done.

Heroes: Pollen, Rowan, Pimenta, Sal

Aegis Global Guild_6 (Corporate)

Certainty in an uncertain world.

A corporate guild that has built a global network spanning crystal refinement, relic acquisition, and rift forecasting. It contracts its breakers to keep supply chains moving and rift activity contained.

Heroes: Niklas, Fiona, Gustav, Dragomir
