Hero Shop Odds

Acts, floors, super shop

One fight per floor, a shop after every fight. Odds depend on the act and on whether it is a super shop. Super shop = the shop after beating the Act 1 boss on floor 6. Lose that fight and it is a normal shop. Floor 12 is the final boss; the run ends there, so there is no shop after it. Red Rift changes enemy stats and adds a shop tax, not hero odds.

Weights

Chance of a rank in one slot = its weight ÷ sum of all weights above 0. Weights at 0 or below are not in the roll. 3 hero slots, each rolled separately.

Pity

Each rank has a counter, starting at 0. Weight = base + counter.

  • Shop built without rank X on offer → X's counter goes up by its step.
  • Rank X appears → X's counter resets to 0, instantly, before the remaining slots roll. Act 1: never two B or two A in one shop.
  • A reroll is a build. 5 rerolls move the counters as much as 5 floors.
  • Counters are independent. Seeing a B does not touch A.
  • Counters persist across floors and are saved with the run. Super shops ignore them and do not move them.

Rule

weight[rank] = base[act][rank] + pity[rank]        (super shop: base only)
P(slot = rank) = weight[rank] ÷ Σ weights above 0
slot rolls X   → pity[X] = 0, weight[X] = base[act][X]
after build    → every rank not shown: pity += step[act][rank]

  • Difficulty changes nothing here.
  • Pool: all 25 heroes minus locked ones (Tilly 50 XP, Zuri 650, Niklas 1250). No repeats within a shop.
