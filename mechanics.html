Mechanics & Constants

Stat & notation key

What the table columns and the shorthand inside ability/relic text mean. Damage and scaling logic verified against the disassembly (see MECHANICS_VERIFIED).

ColumnMeaning
HPMax health.
DefDefense. Incoming damage is divided by (1 + Def/100) — 100 Def ≈ half, 200 ≈ a third. Negative Def amplifies damage instead.
BaseAtkDmgBase auto-attack damage (the ✊ fist stat) — the hero's actual auto-attack hit before modifiers.
AttackA % modifier on auto-attacks: auto damage = BaseAtkDmg × (1 + Attack/100). Usually 0 at base — a bonus stat, not the damage source, which is why most heroes show Attack 0 next to a non-zero BaseAtkDmg.
MagicMagic power. Connects to nothing on its own; abilities scale off it via their own factors.
AtkSpdA % modifier on attack speed.
BaseAS/sBase attacks per second. Actual rate = BaseAS/s × (1 + AtkSpd/100), clamped to 0.2–5; the attack timer is 1 ÷ that.
CritCrit chance in points: chance = min(Crit, 100)/100. Each point above 100 adds +1% crit damage (crit multiplier = 2 + max(Crit − 100, 0)/100).
RangeAttack range. 1 = melee; higher = ranged.
ManaShown as StartingMana / MaxMana + ManaRegen (regen per tick).
Omnivamp / HP/sOmnivamp heals the attacker for finalDamage × OmniVamp/100; HP/s is flat health regen per second (same card slot, whichever is non-zero).

Rank scale: C → B → A → S = ranks 1 → 4 (C is the base/floor at which stats are listed). Events' "rank 1–4" and the rank→rarity table use the same numbers.

Per-rank brackets: a|b|c|d after a stat or effect is its value at rank C|B|A|S. e.g. "150 + 100|200|400|600% Attack" = 150 base damage plus Attack scaled 100 / 200 / 400 / 600% at rank C / B / A / S.

Ability shorthand: "N% Stat" adds N% of that stat to the hit (ability damage = BaseDamage + Σ statValue × rank factor); "Stat/N" is that stat ÷ N (a trailing ×M multiplies the result); "1dN" is a random roll of 1…N.

Keywords (in-game glossary)

Backup: Abilities with Backup also work while the Hero is in the Reserves.

Backup Only: Abilities with Backup Only only work while the Hero is in the Reserves.

Basic Stats: Attack, Max HP, Defense, Crit, Attack Speed, Mana Regen, and Magic are basic stats.

Burn: Characters take damage equal to their Burn each second, then lose one Burn.

Crit: The percent chance that an auto attack or active ability deals double damage. Each Crit above 100 increases Crit Damage by 1%.

Debuff: Poison, Frost, and Burn are Debuffs.

Frost: Characters lose 1 Defense and 1 Attack Speed per 2 Frost they have. Every 2 seconds, characters lose 1 Frost.

Generate: A Shard is generated whenever it is created by an ability, item, or relic.

Grace's The Benevolent permanently gives Heroes the following primary stats for each of their classes:

Assassin: 7 Crit
Duelist: 10 Attack Speed
Mage: 12 Magic
Mystic: 2 Mana Regen
Tank: 15 Defense
Vanguard: 100 Max HP
Warrior: 12 Attack

Lasting: Shields with lasting do not expire over time. Shields normally expire after 10 seconds.

Omnivamp: Restores Health to a character based on the damage they deal. 1 Omnivamp = 1% healing from damage dealt.

Poison: Characters take damage equal to their Poison every 2 seconds.

Each class has a primary stat which increases the effectiveness of their abilities more than others.

Assassin: Crit
Duelist: Attack Speed
Mage: Magic
Mystic: Mana Regen
Tank: Defense
Vanguard: Max HP
Warrior: Attack

Rank: Heroes gain stats and unlock additional abilities as they gain Ranks. Purchasing an additional copy of a Hero from the shop increases their Rank.

Reroll: Players can pay Shards to reroll to shop, displaying new Heroes, items, and relics.

Reserves: Heroes the player owns but are not in combat are kept in the Reserves. Their abilities marked with Backup can still trigger.

Niklas's Red Hot Deals generates Shards equal to 80% of an item's value in the shop. Common = 4 Shards, Rare = 12 Shards, Epic = 20 Shards.

Rush (X): Triggers at the start of combat and expires after X seconds.

Shards: The currency used to purchase upgrades from the shop.

Emergency Rewind protects you from defeat while collecting Shards as you win combats. It collects 5 Shards per combat victory. It is used when a combat is lost, giving you all of the Shards that were accumulated. If you lose another combat while Emergency Rewind is unavailable, your run will end in defeat.

Shield: Damage is removed from a character's Shields before their Health. Shields last 10 seconds unless otherwise specified.

Stall (X): Activates after X seconds have elapsed in combat.

Stealth: A character with Stealth can not be targeted by enemies.

The Rift Storm begins after 50 seconds of combat and deals increasing amounts of damage each second.

Stun: A Stunned character cannot move or act. When Stunned, Heroes gain stacking 25% Stun Resistance for 15 seconds.

Taunt: A Taunted character changes their target to the enemy who Taunted them.

Fiona's The Right Hand creates rarer items as Fiona's rank increases. Rank B = Common, Rank A = Rare, Rank S = Epic.

Niklas's Wheel and Deal creates rarer items as his rank increases. Rank B = Common, Rank A = Rare, Rank S = Epic.
