Red Hot Deals

When to feed Niklas your expensive items

After every fight he survives, Niklas eats the item in his leftmost slot. He refunds 80% of its price and keeps a share of its stats forever. The refund never changes. The share he keeps grows with his rank, from half at C to more than the whole item at S.

Shop phase Put an item in slot 1

He gets an extra item slot. Only the leftmost slot is eaten. Leave it empty and nothing is eaten.

Combat He survives the fight

The item works at full strength during the fight. If Niklas dies, nothing is eaten.

After combat Item is eaten

+Rank % of its stats stay on Niklas permanently, plus 4 / 12 / 20 for Common / Rare / Epic.

The deal vs. the shop

Niklas refunds more than the shop pays when you sell, at every rank. The stats are extra.

The refund is fixed by rarity. It does not depend on his rank or on what you paid, so a 25%-off Epic refunds more than it cost. Never sell an item you could feed him instead.

The playbook

When to feed him expensive items

1
Wait for rank A before feeding Epics

The refund is the same at every rank, but an Epic eaten at C gives half the stats it gives at A. Until he ranks up, feed Commons or leave slot 1 empty.

2
Keep good items out of slot 1

Items in any other slot are never eaten and still give full stats. Move the Epic into slot 1 the shop after he reaches A or S.

3
At S, feed the priciest item every fight

Every meal is worth 125% of the item for 20% of its price. The only real cost now is the fight it takes.

4
Feed plain stat items

Only the item's stats carry over. Item effects, like a Glyph's class bonus, are lost when it is eaten. Epics with only stats, like Scroll of Perseverance, lose nothing.

Fine print

  • Share kept = 25% + 25% per rank, counting C as 1: C 50%, B 75%, A 100%, S 125%.
  • Rank is read when the item is eaten, not when it was bought. Each stat is multiplied and rounded down, so a Defense +45 item at B gives +33.
  • Refunds: Common 4, Rare 12, Epic 20. Any other rarity, including Unique items, refunds 4.
  • Wheel and Deal needs The Dealer spec. Its cast counter carries over between fights. Specs unlock at rank B, so there is no item at rank C.
  • His Rush Burn is 1 + Max HP/500 per equipped item. Eating leaves a slot empty until you refill it, but the Max HP he keeps raises the Burn.
  • The Hoarder spec deals damage per Shard generated this game, and his refunds count as generated Shards. The Executive adds a slot and Shields per equipped item.
