How Kripp opens a Red Rift run

Same study for another streamer: DespairedONE

Every Red Rift run starts on one screen: three heroes, each bundled with a random starting bonus, plus boss tokens to reroll the lot. We went through five of nl_Kripp's Guildrun streams (Aug 29, Aug 30, Aug 31, Sep 1, Sep 13) and recorded every offer, every reroll and every pick: 93 runs in all.

Hover or tap any bonus name to read what it does.

What he does

1

The bonus decides; the hero breaks close calls

We gave every hero and every bonus a single "how much he likes it" number, fitted so that the option he picked scores highest. Then we tested it the honest way: for each stream, we fitted the numbers on the other streams and asked which option he would take. Knowing only the heroes gets 53% of his picks right. Adding the bonuses lifts that to 84%. The bonuses alone manage 76%. Guessing at random would get 33%.

His hero tier list

The top of the list is Rowan. The bottom is Fiona, Ratna, Aria, Pollen. By class, the gap is stark. Counting every time a hero of that class was in the offer he picked from, he took a Mystic 4 of 34 times, a Mage 6 of 36, and an Assassin 22 of 51.

Vampirism is worth passing on a better hero

Bonuses move him more than heroes do. Vampirism was in the final offer 18 times and he took it 16. At the bottom are bonuses he never took: Item Windfall, Quality over Quantity, Relic Bazaar, Quantity over Quality. In 28 runs he passed on a hero he rates higher, and 7 of those were for Fast Track. It works the other way too: in 27 of the 28, the better hero he skipped came with a bonus he avoids.

Try it: what would he pick?

Set up a starting screen and see how the model splits his choice. It starts on a real run where Fast Track beat a better hero.

How far to trust this
  • One player and 93 runs. These are his habits, not rules of the game.
  • The accuracy figures are out of sample: each stream was predicted by numbers fitted without it. The tier lists themselves use every run, and heroes or bonuses seen only a few times have shaky values.
  • 60 confirmed wins and 28 confirmed losses. Spread over this many heroes and bonuses, that is too few to say which picks win more.
