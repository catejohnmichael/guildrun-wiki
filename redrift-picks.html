How a top streaker opens a Red Rift run

Every Red Rift run starts on one screen: three heroes, each bundled with a random starting bonus, plus boss tokens to reroll the lot. We went through seven of DespairedONE's win-streak streams (Sep 21, Sep 22, Sep 23, Sep 24, Sep 25, Sep 26, Sep 27) and recorded every offer, every reroll and every pick: 72 runs in all.

What he does

1

The hero decides; the bonus breaks close calls

We gave every hero and every bonus a single "how much he likes it" number, fitted so that the option he picked scores highest. Then we tested it the honest way: for each stream, we fitted the numbers on the other streams and asked which option he would take. Knowing only the heroes gets 81% of his picks right. Adding the bonuses lifts that to 85%. The bonuses alone manage 60%. Guessing at random would get 33%.

His hero tier list

The top of the list is Rip, Dragomir, Yuuna and Hoyoung. The bottom is Fiona, Aria, Grace, Funke. By class, the gap is stark. Counting every time a hero of that class was in the offer he picked from, he took a Mystic 0 of 30 times, a Mage 1 of 31, and an Assassin 30 of 43.

Item Bazaar is worth passing on a better hero

Most bonuses barely move him, but a few do. Item Bazaar was in the final offer 18 times and he took it 12. At the bottom are bonuses he never took: Mystery Charm, Quantity over Quality, Relic Bazaar. In 9 runs he passed on a hero he rates higher, and 5 of those were for Item Bazaar. It works the other way too: in 8 of the 9, the better hero he skipped came with a bonus he avoids.

Try it: what would he pick?

Set up a starting screen and see how the model splits his choice. It starts on a real run where Item Bazaar beat a better hero.

How far to trust this
  • One player and 72 runs. These are his habits, not rules of the game.
  • The accuracy figures are out of sample: each stream was predicted by numbers fitted without it. The tier lists themselves use every run, and heroes or bonuses seen only a few times have shaky values.
  • Only 1 confirmed loss in the sample (at streak 34, opened with Rip + Shard Maximizer), so nothing here says which picks win more.
