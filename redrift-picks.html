How a top streaker opens a Red Rift run

Every Red Rift run starts on one screen: three heroes, each bundled with a random starting bonus, plus boss tokens to reroll the lot. We went through two of DespairedONE's win-streak streams (Sep 22 and Sep 26) and recorded every offer, every reroll and every pick: 26 runs in all.

A three-step rule predicts 21 of 26 picks

Look at the final screen and take the first of these that is on it:

  1. Rip
  2. An Assassin (Dragomir, Hoyoung, Yuuna…)
  3. A Vanguard (Ming, Rowan, Niklas)

The 5 picks that break it are the interesting ones: twice Fast Track pulled him to a hero outside the list, twice he settled for Tilly, and once a better bonus won out between two Vanguards.

5

The bonus rides along, except Fast Track

His 26 picks used 10 different heroes but 16 different bonuses: the hero is the anchor. Mystery Charm reached the final screen 6 times and was taken 0; Item Bazaar was taken 4 of 6. A "taken" bonus mostly came with the hero he wanted anyway. The exception is Fast Track, an instant rank-up for its hero. He took it 3 of the 5 times it was on the final screen, and 2 of those were on heroes he otherwise never picks.

How far to trust this
  • One player and 26 runs. These are his habits, not rules of the game.
  • Only 1 loss in the sample (at streak 34, opened with Rip + Shard Maximizer), so nothing here says which picks win more.
  • The three-step rule and the "core hero" grouping were read off these same runs. The real test is whether they predict the next stream.
