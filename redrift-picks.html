How three top players open a Red Rift run

Every Red Rift run starts on one screen: three heroes, each bundled with a random starting bonus, plus boss tokens to reroll the lot. We went through 17 streams by three players and recorded every offer, every reroll and every pick: 217 runs and 347 offers in all. Then we asked what they agree on, where they part ways, and how well one player's habits predict another's.

Three ways to pick

For each player we gave every hero and every bonus a single "how much he likes it" number, fitted so that the option he picked scores highest, and tested it stream by stream on streams it hadn't seen. Some things are common ground. Every one of them values Rowan, Niklas and Tilly, and every one of them rates Vampirism, Fast Track, Starter Bundle, Shard Pouch and All In as a plus. Rerolls and pace

Kripp and dillyg10 decide fast: a median 8 and 9 seconds, with 70% and 75% of picks made inside 15 seconds. DespairedONE's picks split in two: 29% come inside 15 seconds, but 53% take a minute or more; his streams are billed as educational, with commentary on each choice. How far to trust this
  • Three players and 217 runs. These are their habits, not rules of the game, and a hero or bonus seen only a few times has a shaky value.
  • Accuracy figures are out of sample: a player's own figure comes from streams the model didn't see, and cross-player figures use a model fitted only on the other player.
  • Values are fitted per player, and the player with fewer runs gets values pulled a little toward zero. Compare colours across columns as "likes / indifferent / avoids", not to the decimal.
  • Kripp's streams are from Aug 29 to Sep 13; the other two are from Sep 21 to 28. If the game changed in between, some of Kripp's differences could be the patch rather than the player.
  • Wins and losses are only counted where the next recorded run proves them, and there are too few losses to say which openings win more.

How this was collected. The VODs were scanned automatically for the choose-hero screen, and hero and bonus names were read off each offer; each reroll spends one boss token, which is how rerolls are told apart. Only Red Rift runs are counted (dillyg10 also plays regular difficulty). The pick was confirmed from the relic that appears in the run's relic bar a few seconds later, or from the hero portrait in the party bar when two offered bonuses share an icon or the bonus gives an item instead of a relic. Every pick was then checked by eye against the video. Win streaks come from the pre-run screen: the leaderboard line for DespairedONE and Kripp, and the big "current win streak" number, read by eye, for dillyg10. A streak marked * couldn't be seen and was filled in from the runs either side.
